Our Story

Built by people who've bought the fakes and learned the hard way.

Rather than build another marketplace where anyone can list anything, we built a smaller, slower model: every item is physically inspected by us before it goes live, priced fairly for both sides, and shipped the way it should be.

What We Stand For

Two rules we don't bend on

Authenticity is non-negotiable

If we can't verify a piece with confidence, it doesn't get listed — full stop, regardless of how good a sale it would be.

Condition is described, not sold

Flaws are photographed and disclosed upfront. What you see in the listing is what arrives at your door.

Frequently Asked
How do I know an item is genuinely authenticated?

Every listing includes a condition report and authentication note. Higher-value items also carry third-party verification on request.

Do you ship pan-India?

Yes — insured courier shipping is available across all serviceable pin codes, with doorstep verification for high-value lots.

What's your return policy?

Returns are accepted within 48 hours of delivery if the item doesn't match its listed condition report.
